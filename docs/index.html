Parking at ticketed events, booked and paid for in advance.

GatePass is a marketplace for parking at public ticketed events. Event organizers list the parking capacity available at a venue; drivers reserve and pay for a spot before they arrive; GatePass issues a QR pass that gets scanned at the gate. No searching for parking, no cash at the barrier.

01

Organizer lists capacity

Venues and event organizers publish available parking slots for an event, with pricing and time windows.

02

Driver books & pays

Drivers reserve a spot in the GatePass app and pay online ahead of the event — no on-site negotiation.

03

QR pass at the gate

A QR pass is issued on booking and scanned at entry, confirming the reservation instantly.

Who this is for

Drivers attending concerts, sports fixtures, exhibitions and other ticketed public events where parking is limited and normally first-come-first-served. Event organizers and venue operators who want to monetize and manage parking capacity instead of leaving it informal.

Get the app

GatePass is built with Expo/React Native for drivers. The organizer dashboard is in development.
