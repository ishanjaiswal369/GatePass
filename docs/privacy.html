Privacy Policy

Last updated: [date you publish this]

What we collect

When you create a GatePass account we collect your email address, and the name and vehicle details you add to your profile. When you make a booking we collect the event, parking slot, and payment details needed to confirm and honour that booking. With your permission, the app also reads your device location to guide you to the venue and confirm arrival at the gate.

How we use it

  • To create and authenticate your account (email sign-in codes).
  • To process and confirm parking bookings, and issue your QR pass.
  • To process payments through our payment gateway partner.
  • To contact you about a booking, a refund, or a change to an event.

Payment data

Payments are processed by our payment gateway partner (Cashfree Payments). GatePass does not store your card, UPI, or bank account details on its own servers — that information is handled directly by the payment gateway.

Sharing

We share only what's needed with the event organizer whose parking you book (name and vehicle number, for gate verification) and with our payment gateway partner, to process a transaction. We do not sell your personal data.

Data retention & deletion

You can request deletion of your account and associated data from within the app, or by contacting us (see Contact). Booking records may be retained as required for accounting, tax and dispute-resolution purposes.
