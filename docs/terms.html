The service

GatePass ("we", "us") operates a marketplace that lets event organizers list parking capacity at ticketed public events, and lets drivers reserve and pay for a parking spot in advance. On booking, GatePass issues a QR pass that is scanned for entry at the venue's parking gate.

Eligibility & accounts

You must provide a valid email address to create an account and complete sign-in with the code sent to it. You're responsible for bookings made from your account.

Bookings & payment

A booking is confirmed once payment is completed via our payment gateway partner (Cashfree Payments). Prices are set by the event organizer / venue and may vary by event and time slot. See our Refund & Cancellation Policy for cancellation terms.

Your QR pass

The QR pass is valid only for the event, date, and vehicle it was issued for, and only for a single entry unless stated otherwise. Do not share your pass — a scanned/used pass cannot be reused.

Organizer responsibility

GatePass facilitates the booking and payment; the parking space itself is provided and managed by the event organizer / venue. GatePass is not responsible for damage to a vehicle while parked, or for on-site conduct at the venue.
